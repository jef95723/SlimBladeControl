# SlimBlade Control v0.6.2

Mappings now reconnect automatically after USB unplug/replug, sleep/wake, screen lock/unlock, and user-session switching. Temporary interruptions show Waiting and recover using the last applied settings, with no Start click required. Transient device or input-access failures retry approximately every two seconds while mappings remain enabled.

- Manual Pause stays paused through reconnects and session changes. Choose Start to resume; Quit exits completely.
- Held keys, modifiers, and clicks are released on interruption. Pending combinations are cleared, and reconnected buttons arm after a neutral report.
- Recovery preserves your applied mappings and motion settings without applying unfinished UI edits.
- Repeated and stale device/session callbacks are guarded against duplicate or unwanted reconnections.

The build passes all seven Rust tests, 640,000 randomized C/Rust transitions, and native regression tests, including the new reconnect lifecycle scenarios. The author confirmed automatic recovery after USB unplug/replug and screen lock/unlock on the actual trackball, and the installed app returned to Active. Sleep/wake and fast-user-switch event sequences are covered by automated tests; live checks for those cases remain outstanding.

The Apple Silicon app remains ad-hoc signed and not notarized. After upgrading, macOS may require removing and re-adding Accessibility and Input Monitoring entries for `/Applications/SlimBlade Control.app`, then relaunching. No additional permission, helper, or login item is required.
